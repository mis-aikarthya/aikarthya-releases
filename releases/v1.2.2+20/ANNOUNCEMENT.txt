*Good Evening Team,*

We are excited to share that *v1.2.2+20 is now live* with some of our biggest features yet. This update introduces:

🤖 *Aikarthya Agent (Web)* — Your own AI assistant, right inside the app. Console users will see the Agent bubble on the App/Home — open it to ask questions about your schools, observations and reports in plain language. ⚙️ *Reporting AI Settings* — Management can now configure which AI model each workflow uses, including the Aikarthya Agent, from the Settings page under Reports.

🏛️ *School Closures* — Log closures as date ranges (not just single days) and mark SA/FA exam kinds directly.
